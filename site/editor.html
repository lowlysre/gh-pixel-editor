How painting works

You paint legend characters, not colours. Each character maps to one xterm-256 colour, or to a ramp that lighting shades across. To change a character's colour, pick a palette colour, then click that character's swatch in the legend.

Previews

Solid is what to put in CI logs. Half looks sharper in a terminal but smears in GitHub's log viewer.
